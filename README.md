# OpenFour

Four-bar live intro desk for bedroom producers and AI-music finishers.

Suno and loop-based DAWs dump straight into the first verse. Session players write four live bars of walk-in: kit establish, bass root, nylon figure, piano door — then the verse starts. OpenFour seats live chairs on that cut, plays pocket vs open, and exports a punch list.

Live samples only — FluidR3 acoustic grand, upright bass, nylon guitar, trumpet, violin, and an acoustic kit from the PreEight sample CDN. No oscillators. Audio never leaves the tab.

## Distinct from yesterday and the rest of the line

| Tool | Job |
| --- | --- |
| PreEight | Eight bars *before* the hook (pre-chorus climb) |
| LiftTwo | Two bars of climb *into* the hook |
| TagFour | Four-bar *last-line tag* that ends the record |
| AfterHook | Eight bars *after* the hook as a floor / vamp |
| EndEight | Last eight bars of the record |
| LastHook | Last chorus is the money chorus |
| ChickFour | Four bars of muted-electric chicka (yesterday) |
| PivotFour / PizzFour / HarpFour / PedalFour | Turnaround, pizz, harp, pedal-point colour |
| **OpenFour** | Four bars that *open* the record so the first verse has a door |

Yesterday’s ship was ChickFour (muted electric right-hand). OpenFour is the missing front door: the four bars before bar 1 of the verse. Not a pre-chorus, not a lift, not a tag.

## Loop

1. Pick a groove (Amber Walk, Porch Climb, Fold Radio).
2. Hear A: eight bars of verse. It loops. No door.
3. Stamp an open recipe (walk-in, kit establish, nylon door, piano door, bass root, hat open, brass call, violin hold, half hush, stop hit).
4. Hear B: four of open + eight of verse. Playback stops.
5. Copy the punch list. Drop the idea on bars 1–4 of the arrangement.

Space plays B. A is verse loop. 4 is open only.
